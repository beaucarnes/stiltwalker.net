## Document 2: Pre-Show 14-Point Hardware & Wardrobe Safety Inspection Checklist

- [ ] **1. Upper & Lower Calf Band Bolts:** Locknuts snug; zero thread stripping or ovaled aluminum holes.
- [ ] **2. 4" Comfort Strap Velcro:** Full hook-and-loop engagement; clean of lint/debris; buckle D-rings intact.
- [ ] **3. Arch & Heel Straps:** Webbing free of fraying at rivet points; heel cup locked in correct shoe-size slot.
- [ ] **4. Height-Extension Pins:** Spring-loaded locking pins fully seated through BOTH inner and outer leg tubes on left and right stilts at identical hole count.
- [ ] **5. Ankle Action Coil Springs:** Equal return tension left/right; nylon bushings intact with no metal-on-metal grinding.
- [ ] **6. Rubber Sole Treads:** Lug pattern deep and free of embedded glass/gravel; countersunk sole screws recessed below rubber contact plane (protects ballroom hardwood/marble!).
- [ ] **7. Trouser Cuff Hem Check (CRITICAL):** While standing mounted, verify pant hem hangs **1.5 to 2.0 inches ABOVE floor level** and inner stirrup/elastic cannot slip underneath the stilt sole.
- [ ] **8. Staging Route Walk-Through:** Walk the route on flat shoes BEFORE rigging up—spot wet bar mats, floor outlets, low exit signs, and sloped ADA ramps.
