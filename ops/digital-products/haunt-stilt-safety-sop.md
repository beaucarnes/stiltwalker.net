## Section 1: Why Haunt Stilt Work Requires Dedicated Protocols
Commercial haunted houses combine **four compounding hazards** rarely present at daytime parades:
1. **Glycol/Water Fog Fluid Condensation:** Creates an invisible, ultra-slick film on painted plywood, concrete, and rubber mats within 45 minutes of show open.
2. **Strobe Disorientation & Darkness:** Destroys depth perception and peripheral foot-placement tracking.
3. **Startled Patron Collisions:** Fight-or-flight guests frequently lunge backward or drop to the floor directly in front of a stilt actor's stride.
4. **Heavy Latex / Quad-Rig Costumes:** Raise center of gravity and trap body heat.

---

## Section 2: Controlled Knee-Tuck Fall Progression (Never "Timber" Fall)
- **Rule #1: Protect Wrists & Clavicles.** Reaching straight stiff arms toward concrete from an 8-foot fall height causes distal radius fractures.
- **Mat Drill Progression (Mandatory Before Live Scare Zones):**
  1. Wear low-profile **hard-shell knee pads** and **skateboard wrist splints** *underneath* stilt trousers on every haunt shift.
  2. When losing balance forward: immediately **flex hips and bend knees deeply** to drop your center of mass by 18–24 inches before ground contact.
  3. Tuck chin to chest, keep elbows slightly bent, slap/roll across forearm-to-shoulder diagonal on crash mats during pre-season training rather than locking straight palms.
  4. **Never fight an unrecoverable backward tip** in a narrow hallway—step aggressively into the nearest braced scenic wall stud at shoulder height or drop into a controlled squat.

## Section 3: Haunt Zone Design Rules for Attractions Managers
- **Dedicated Stilt Lane Marking:** Apply recessed matte UV/glow gaffer tape along floor baseboards marking safe flat footing free of scenic trip props.
- **Fog Nozzle Placement:** Never aim fog chillers directly across a stilt actor's primary lunge mark; place absorbent non-slip industrial drainage matting at lunge resets.
- **15-Minute Hydrate / Un-Strap Rest Cycle:** Maximum 45 minutes continuous rigging in masks/latex before mandatory 15-minute un-strapped calf circulation recovery.
